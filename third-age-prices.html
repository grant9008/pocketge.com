What changed

A Grand Exchange offer used to be capped at 2,147,483,647 gp — max cash. Anything worth more than that simply could not be bought or sold through the exchange, so the most expensive 3rd age pieces traded hand-to-hand and had no official price at all. The GE Improvements: Beyond Max Cash update raised that ceiling on 30 September 2026, and those items are now ordinary tradeable goods with real Grand Exchange prices behind them.

A freshly uncapped item only gets a price once somebody actually trades one. On a piece worth billions that can take days, and the very first prints tend to be wide — one motivated buyer is not a market. Treat early numbers on the rarest pieces as a starting point rather than a valuation.

Why some pieces are worth so much more than others

3rd age is a single cosmetic set on paper, but the market treats it as several. The druidic pieces and the pickaxe come from the hardest sources and are held in the smallest numbers, so they carry the biggest prices. The commoner armour pieces turn over far more often and price accordingly. Rarity does the work here, not stats — 3rd age gear is collected and worn, not optimised into a setup.

Flipping 3rd age

These are the thinnest items on the exchange. A single trade can move a quoted price by a noticeable percentage, and days can pass between prints, so the spread you see is not the spread you will get. The 2% GE tax applies to the sell side like anything else, and on a multi-billion item that tax is itself a large number worth doing the arithmetic on. PocketGE's target prices and after-tax margins account for it on every item page.
