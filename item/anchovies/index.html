About Anchovies

In game they are examined as “Some nicely cooked anchovies.” Anchovies are available to free-to-play accounts. The base game value is 15 gp, which is what shops price from rather than what players pay.

Anchovies buy limit

The Grand Exchange lets one account buy 15,000 every 4 hours. That is a generous limit, so the market rather than the limit is usually what caps a position here. Filling it once costs about 360,000 gp at the 24 gp price recorded on 20 September 2026. The limit is per account and resets on a rolling 4-hour timer.

GE tax on Anchovies

The 2% sale tax is rounded down, and at 24 gp it rounds to nothing — sales under 50 gp are untaxed, so a flip here keeps its whole margin.

High alching Anchovies

High alchemy turns Anchovies into 9 gp. That is less than the 133 gp nature rune the cast costs, so alching Anchovies always loses money.

How much Anchovies trade

Anchovies see about 41.2K trades a day, which makes them steadily traded — an order priced at the spread normally fills the same hour. That is roughly 3 times the 15,000 buy limit.

Anchovies FAQ

How much are Anchovies in OSRS? Anchovies are 24 gp on the Grand Exchange as of 20 September 2026, with an insta-sell of 24 gp. The chart above is live.

What is the buy limit for Anchovies? 15,000 every 4 hours.

Can you high alch Anchovies for profit? No — the alch value is 9 gp and the nature rune alone costs 133 gp.

Do you need members for Anchovies? No, free-to-play accounts can buy and sell them.
