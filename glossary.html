OSRS GE Badge & Signal Glossary

Every badge, marker and tag the PocketGE terminal can put on a row, a chart or a price — and what each one is actually telling you. This is the same glossary the app's Help button opens, on a page of its own so it can be read, linked and searched without opening the terminal first.

Row highlights
▲ 5D HIGHAt or near the 5-day high — a notable peak across the last 5 days, not just an intraday wiggle. Brighter glow signals the macro move.
▼ 5D LOWAt or near the 5-day low — a notable trough across the last 5 days. Stronger signal than a routine day low.
▲ HIGHAt or above its 24-hour high. Sell now to catch the peak. Row glows RuneScape-green.
▼ LOWAt or below its 24-hour low. Buy now to catch the dip. Row glows RuneScape-gold.
Browser notifications
▲ BREAKBrowser notifications only fire when a favorite's live price BREAKS PAST its tracked 24h or 5D extreme — a genuine new high/low, not a lingering "near the edge" state. Watermarks update automatically so the next alert needs an even bigger move. 5D breaks are flagged separately because they're rarer / bigger deal.
Margin tags
+1.2M eaEstimated per-item margin from the live insta-buy / insta-sell spread, after the 2% GE tax.
+450K ea avg30-day average per-item margin (for 1M lists). More stable than a one-day snapshot.
Favorite reorder
DragDrag anywhere on a favorite's row to move it up or down the list; the order is saved. Tap 🗑 on a row to remove it, or swipe-left on touch.
Chart markers
Lows (gold) — insta-sell prints, the price sellers actually accepted. Highs (teal) — insta-buy prints, what buyers paid.
Period Low / Period High — solid dot with a pale centre. The lowest and highest price across the whole timeframe you're viewing.
Recent Low / Recent High — hollow ring. The extreme within just the most recent quarter of the window, so you can tell an old peak from a fresh one.
Trend — the smoothed mid-price line. Follows direction without the print-to-print noise.
BarsTraded volume, gold for insta-sells and teal for insta-buys. On short screens they sit faintly behind the price instead of in their own strip, to leave the chart more room. Tap any point on the chart for its exact price and volume.
Analyst Rating (sidebar gauge — the one opinion on the page)
STRONG BUY … STRONG SELLOne call, scored against the selected chart timeframe's price range: near the window's floor with a real margin reads Buy; near its ceiling reads Sell. Mid-range gets no word at all — the meter's needle sits centred and that is the whole answer, so nothing is put on the line above it pretending to be a call. Underneath, the item is graded twice — once as a buy, once as a sell — and how far apart those two scores land is what decides how strong the word is. A near-tie means the item flips about as well in either direction, so it shows the centred needle rather than picking a side. Expand the gauge to see both scores and the four things that produced them. Because each timeframe has its own price range, the same price can rate differently on 1D and 6M — that's the window changing, not the item. "Buy the Dip" / "Sell the Rip" mean the setup is forming but the price hasn't reached the target level yet. Advisory only — the Target / Swing prices are the actual trade.
Recommended Flip (the ⚡ card)
SCORE / 100How good this particular flip looks right now — not a rating of the item itself. Two things drive it: the net edge after tax (full marks at a 3% edge, and it stops counting above that, so a 12% edge scores the same as a 3% one) and liquidity (full marks at 10M traded a day). If one side hasn't traded recently the score takes a 20% cut. Hover or tap the score on the card to see the exact arithmetic.
THINSOLIDSTRONGPRIMEThin under 55 · Solid 55+ · Strong 70+ · Prime 85+. Because the finder only ever offers flips it has already checked are viable and fillable, a shown pick starts at 20 points before edge or volume are counted — so in practice you'll see the upper bands far more often than Thin. Tap ↻ Next to walk down the ranking; the score falls as the picks get thinner.
Target prices
Target BuyShown on the 1D timeframe — the day-flip bid. 15th-percentile of insta-sell prints in the last ~90 minutes (wall-clock), nudged toward the very-recent realised low. For high-volatility items (24h range >4%) the anchor blends toward 0.5% above the 24h low, so a patient bid can catch multi-hour dips.
Target SellShown on the 1D timeframe — the day-flip ask. -1 below the 85th-percentile of insta-buy prints in the last ~90 minutes, floored at live insta-buy so we never recommend selling below where buyers are paying right now. For high-volatility items the anchor blends toward 0.5% below the 24h high. If the live spread can't beat the 2% tax, both 1D boxes switch to the day's realized dip/peak (recency-weighted percentiles of the day's prints) — the levels worth placing patient orders at.
Swing Buy / SellOn 5D and longer timeframes the boxes switch to swing anchors for THAT window — day trade vs swing trade. Swing Buy sits near the window's realised floor, Swing Sell near its ceiling, using recency-weighted percentiles: a high printed last week counts in full, one from months ago barely counts — so a downtrending item won't quote you a sell level the market left behind. Never the absolute min/max, so one freak spike can't set your target. Longer horizons never quote a worse level than shorter ones — the 1M ask is always at least the 5D and 1D ask, because more patience can always execute the shorter plan. The ask is then held to what's actually placeable: it can't exceed the higher of local resistance (where sellers have been filling over the last ~30 days) and a sanity cap over the live price — 15% on items above 1M, 25% above 100k, uncapped on cheap items that genuinely swing that hard, and doubled when the window is clearly trending up. Without it a year-long slide quotes an ask from the top of the slide, a level the market has left behind. The bid gets no such ceiling: bidding low is always placeable, it just may not fill. Both are defaults — the −/+ buttons override them with no limit. The grade beside each number judges the same window.
GRADE 0–100The score beside each target rates the current price against that same window, and carries a word with it: Steal Buy / Top Sell at 85+, Strong Entry / Strong Exit at 70+, OK at 50+, Weak at 30+, Avoid below that. You'll also see Wait for Dip / Wait for Peak when the setup is forming but the price hasn't arrived, Thin — Hold when the margin is too slim to be worth it, and No Margin when the spread can't beat the tax.
Headline numbers
POTENTIAL PROFITThe per-item margin after the 2% tax, multiplied by the item's 4-hour buy limit — what one full cycle could clear if it all fills, not a per-item figure. Tap it to open the breakdown and edit the quantity.
30-DAY RANGEThe slider under the chart places the live price between its 30-day low and high. Near the left means it's cheap against the last month; near the right means it's near the top of it.
Bank of Gielinor
Stash whatever you're holding — just qty is required, cost is optional. Total Wealth headline shows the live insta-sell value of every stack net of the 2% GE tax. Add a cost basis to track growth; set ±% for swing notifications.
